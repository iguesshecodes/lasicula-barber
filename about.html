The Founder

Dr. Radicia.

La Sicula Barber was founded by Dr. Radicia, a formally trained barber who completed his studies at the London School of Barbering — one of the UK's most respected and rigorous barbering academies. His training covered all cutting disciplines: clipper work, scissor technique, wet shaving, and beard sculpting.

The name La Sicula reflects the founder's Italian heritage and the precision that Italian barbering culture has represented for generations. That precision is not a marketing claim. It is the standard that every client experiences from the moment they sit in the chair.

"The chair does not care who sits in it. The standard never drops."

Dr. Radicia — Founder, La Sicula Barber
